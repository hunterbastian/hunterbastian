# Inspiration

Reference images Hunter shared. Notes are what they point at for meadow.

- `2026-09-27-tiny-building-game-walls.png`: a post by @anastasiaopara (Rust + Bevy, March 2022) showing her tiny building game: a curved dry-stone wall drawn across a lush, wind-blown grassy hill, a dirt path running through an arch in the wall, and a dashed ring cursor on the ground. This is the seed of meadow. Takeaways: **drawing a wall is one gesture** (drag and it builds itself), chunky rounded bricks with a ragged top, paths that turn into arches where they cross a wall, dense soft grass that swallows the wall's footing, a pale overcast sky, and the calm "just play with it" toy feel.
