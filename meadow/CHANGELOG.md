# meadow Changelog

Plain-language log of what changed, newest first.

## Sunday, September 27, 2026

**In the game**
- **meadow is born:** a grassy hill under a pale sky, with a curved stone wall and a dirt path running through an arch to start you off.
- **Walls:** drag to draw a dry-stone wall. It follows your stroke, lays its bricks in staggered courses, and finishes with a ragged, gap-toothed top. Hold Shift for a straight wall; `[` and `]` (or − / +) make walls lower or taller.
- **Paths and arches:** paint dirt paths with little pebbles; the grass parts around them. Wherever a wall crosses a path, it opens an arch with a ring of fanned stones.
- **Eraser:** cuts walls where you sweep it (leaving the pieces standing) and clears path paint.
- **Grass:** hundreds of thousands of blades swaying in the wind, patchy greens and straw.
- **Pixels:** the whole scene is drawn in chunky pixels with dithering and a cozy palette. Change the pixel size or turn dithering and the palette off in the ☀ panel; P toggles pixels.
- **Undo and redo**, and your meadow saves itself as you build.
- **Sun and wind** sliders, a help card (H), an FPS meter (F).
- **Phones:** one finger builds, two fingers look around. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen app with a little pixel-art icon.

**Behind the scenes**
- Vite + TypeScript + Three.js, set up like VARA: unit tests, headless UI tests, Vercel config and analytics, and project notes (CLAUDE.md, ROADMAP.md, HANDOFF.md).
