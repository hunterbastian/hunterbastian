Kohan

One autumn tree on a spit of land, a cove, a standing stone in the shallows and a lantern on the islet. Walk it in first person, through a PS2 and GameCube post stack.

  • W A S D walk
  • Mouse look
  • Shift run
  • Space hop
  • E use
  • Tab post stack
  • 1–7 passes
  • P presets
  • Left thumb walk, push far to run
  • Right thumb look

  1. Render small, then scale up.

  2. Shrink the frame, Kawase blur, add it back.

  3. Blend toward the blur with distance.

  4. Radial blur of the bright sky toward the sun.

  5. Fewer colors, ordered dither, grain.

  6. Blend each line with the lines above and below.

  7. Cable color bleed, scanlines, aperture grille.

Pass order follows gsimone's thread on recreating the PS2 and GameCube look.
